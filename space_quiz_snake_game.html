Comet

An object made of ice, dust, and rock. When it gets close to the Sun, its ice heats up and forms a bright coma and tail.

Meteor

A streak of light seen when a small space rock enters Earth's atmosphere and burns up.

Asteroid

A rocky or metallic object that orbits the Sun. Most are found in the asteroid belt between Mars and Jupiter.
